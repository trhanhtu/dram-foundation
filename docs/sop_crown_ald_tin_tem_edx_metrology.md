# SOP: Crown Sidewall ALD TiN Conformal Step Coverage TEM/EDX Metrology

- **Date**: 2026-10-15
- **Source**: Domain Expert AI (DRAM Foundation VN)
- **Status**: Implemented
- **Generation**: Gen 3 (180nm DRAM Node)
- **Action Type**: Make

## Overview & Rationale
In 180nm stacked capacitor-over-bitline (COB) architectures utilizing 3D high-aspect-ratio crown geometries (capacitance budget >= 25 to 30 fF per cell over W-plug contacts with Ta2O5/Al2O3 or ONO dielectrics), atomic layer deposition (ALD) of the TiN top metal electrode must achieve near-perfect conformal step coverage. Inadequate bottom or sidewall step coverage induces localized electric field crowding, increases leakage current above the 1 fA/cell threshold, and degrades dielectric reliability. This SOP defines high-resolution cross-sectional Transmission Electron Microscopy (TEM) and Energy Dispersive X-ray Spectroscopy (EDX) nanoprobe metrology to quantify physical step coverage and elemental stoichiometry along the 3D crown sidewalls.

## Key Details / Metrology Workflow
- Dual-beam FIB lamella preparation with protective capping.
- Calibrated HR-TEM / HAADF-STEM imaging at 200 kV.
- Cross-sectional thickness profiling across top, mid-sidewall, and bottom crown corners.
- STEM-EDX line scan and spectrum quantification for Ti:N stoichiometry and halogen (Cl) residue.
