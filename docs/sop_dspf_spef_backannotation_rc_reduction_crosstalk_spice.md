# Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)
## DSPF/SPEF Back-Annotation, RC Mesh Reduction & Bitline-to-Bitline Coupling Crosstalk SPICE Analysis

- **Document ID:** SOP-DRAM-180NM-RC-SPICE-002
- **Node / Generation:** Gen 2 (180 nm / 0.18 µm)
- **Action Type:** Make
- **Context:** 180nm Open-Source DRAM Chip (DRAM Foundation VN) > 180nm 1T1C DRAM Cell Bitline & Wordline Matrix Layout > DRC/LVS Verification & Parasitic Extraction for Bitline/Wordline Matrix > PEX Extraction & Wordline/Bitline Parasitic RC Modeling > DSPF/SPEF Back-Annotation, RC Mesh Reduction & Bitline-to-Bitline Coupling Crosstalk SPICE Analysis

---

### 1. Purpose & Scope
This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) defines the protocol for back-annotating extracted parasitic netlists (DSPF/SPEF) into the transistor-level schematic of a 180 nm 1T1C folded bitline DRAM memory matrix, performing algorithmic $RC$ mesh reduction (TICER/pole-zero truncation), and executing transient multi-bitline SPICE simulations to analyze inter-bitline coupling crosstalk, charge sharing, and differential sense amplifier signal development ($\Delta V$).

---

### 2. Verified Literature Citations & Physical Constraints
All electrical targets, layout pitches, and device parameters directly adhere to verified 180 nm literature context:
- **[180nm DRAM Cell Layout Topology: Stacked Capacitor vs Deep Trench]**:
  - Access transistor channel dimensions: Channel length $L = 180\text{ nm}$, width $W = 220\text{ nm}$.
  - Unit cell layout: $8F^2$ and $6F^2$ folded bitline layout with Stacked Capacitor Over Bitline (COB).
  - Storage node capacitance budget: Minimum $C_s \ge 28\text{ fF/cell}$ (Crown/MIM polysilicon or metal structure with ONO or $\text{Ta}_2\text{O}_5 / \text{Al}_2\text{O}_3$ high-$k$ stack, $\kappa > 15$).
  - Contact architecture: Bitline contact shared between two adjacent memory cells via tungsten plug ($W$-plug).
  - Target leakage: Sub-threshold and junction leakage $< 1\text{ fA/cell}$ at $85^\circ\text{C}$.
- **[Folded Bitline Sense Amplifier Circuit Design for DRAM Cell Readout]**:
  - Folded bitline sense amplifier: CMOS cross-coupled inverter latch between complementary bitlines $BL$ and $\overline{BL}$.
  - Bitline parasitic capacitance baseline: Nominal total bitline capacitance $C_{BL} \approx 250\text{ fF}$.
  - Precharge level: Equalized to $V_{DD}/2$ before wordline activation.
  - Signal development formula:
    $$\Delta V = \left(\frac{C_s}{C_{BL} + C_s}\right) \cdot \left(V_{cell} - \frac{V_{DD}}{2}\right)$$
    For typical $C_s = 25\text{ fF}$ and $C_{BL} = 250\text{ fF}$, readout differential voltage $\Delta V \approx 100\text{ mV to } 150\text{ mV}$.
- **[Legacy 180nm DRAM Architecture & Cell Capacitor Engineering]**:
  - Array pitch: Bitline and wordline pitch = $360\text{ nm to } 400\text{ nm}$.
  - Storage capacitance range: $25\text{ fF to } 30\text{ fF}$ per cell to ensure retention $> 64\text{ ms}$ at $85^\circ\text{C}$.
  - Gate dielectric: Thermally grown $\text{SiO}_2$ with physical thickness $\sim 3.5\text{ nm}$ ($850^\circ\text{C}\text{--}900^\circ\text{C}$ dry oxidation).

### 3. Step-by-Step Operating Procedure

#### Step 1: Format Parsing & DSPF/SPEF Integrity Validation
1. Parse the extracted parasitic file (DSPF/SPEF) generated by the 3D parasitic extraction engine for the memory array block ($BL_0, \overline{BL_0}, BL_1, \overline{BL_1}, \dots, BL_k$).
2. Verify pin and node naming syntax mapping against schematic netlist:
   - Access transistor gate, source, and drain terminals ($L = 180\text{ nm}$, $W = 220\text{ nm}$).
   - Shared bitline tungsten contact node connections across adjacent cell pairs.
   - Plate reference node (`VPLATE` bias = $V_{DD}/2$).
3. Run DC topological graph consistency checks to ensure no floating nodes or DC shorts exist within the cross-coupled bitline mesh.

#### Step 2: Algorithmic RC Mesh Reduction (TICER & Cutoff Frequency Truncation)
1. Set the target electrical simulation bandwidth based on wordline edge rate ($t_r \approx 1.0\text{ ns}$ to $1.5\text{ ns}$, $f_{max} \ge \frac{0.35}{t_r} \approx 2.5\text{ GHz}$).
2. Execute Time-Constant-based Inductance, Conductance, and Capacitance Elimination and Reduction (TICER) or state-space reduction on distributed $R-C-CC$ segments.
3. Consolidate internal unobservable parasitic nodes while strictly conserving:
   - Total lumped capacitance to substrate ($C_{gnd}$) within $\pm 0.5\%$.
   - Lateral inter-bitline coupling capacitances ($C_{c, BL-BL}$) between adjacent tracks within $\pm 0.5\%$.
   - Total series loop resistance along the full bitline length ($\sim 512$ to $1024$ cells).
4. Output the reduced SPICE subcircuit netlist (`array_reduced_pex.sp`).

#### Step 3: Transistor-Level Back-Annotation & Stimulus Harness Setup
1. Stitch reduced subcircuits with full SPICE BSIM models for the access transistors ($L = 180\text{ nm}$, $W = 220\text{ nm}$, $T_{ox} = 3.5\text{ nm}$).
2. Instantiate physical storage capacitors ($C_s = 28\text{ fF}$) at each cell storage node (`SN`).
3. Back-annotate folded bitline pairs ($BL_i, \overline{BL_i}$) to their respective cross-coupled CMOS latch sense amplifiers and equalization transistors.
4. Configure worst-case crosstalk pattern vectors on an $N$-bitline matrix ($N \ge 5$ adjacent tracks):
   - **Victim pair**: Reading a weak data "0" ($V_{cell} = 0\text{ V}$) or weak data "1" ($V_{cell} = V_{DD} = 1.8\text{ V}$).
   - **Aggressor tracks (In-phase & Out-of-phase)**: Adjacent lines switching concurrently during wordline assertion and sense amplifier latch activation.

#### Step 4: Transient SPICE Readout & Coupling Crosstalk Simulation
1. Execute transient analysis with tight numerical tolerances (`reltol=1e-4`, `vntol=1e-6`, `abstol=1e-12`, integration method `gear` or `trap` with conservative step limit $dt_{max} \le 10\text{ ps}$).
2. Sequence memory phases:
   - Phase 1 (Equalization): Assert `EQ` to equalize $BL$ and $\overline{BL}$ to $V_{DD}/2 = 0.90\text{ V}$.
   - Phase 2 (Isolation): Deassert `EQ` prior to wordline activation.
   - Phase 3 (Charge Sharing): Assert Wordline ($WL$) high ($V_{PP} \approx 2.5\text{ V}$ to $2.8\text{ V}$ over-drive to prevent $V_{th}$ drop through $180\text{ nm}$ access transistor). Cell charge $Q_s = C_s \cdot (V_{cell} - V_{BL})$ dumps onto $BL$.
   - Phase 4 (Sensing & Latch): Fire sense amplifier latch clocks (`SAN` pulling to $0\text{ V}$, `SAP` pulling to $V_{DD}$).
3. Record transient dynamic waveforms on victim and aggressor lines.

#### Step 5: Waveform Extraction & Noise Margin Analysis
1. Measure differential signal development $\Delta V = |V_{BL} - V_{\overline{BL}}|$ at the instant immediately preceding sense amplifier activation ($t_{sense}$).
2. Extract dynamic crosstalk noise voltage spike $\Delta V_{xtalk}$ induced by adjacent aggressor bitlines via coupling capacitance $C_c$.
3. Compute signal degradation ratio:
   $$\eta_{xtalk} = \frac{\Delta V_{xtalk}}{\Delta V_{ideal}} \times 100\%$$
4. Verify sensing margin against minimum sense amplifier input offset voltage ($V_{offset} \le 20\text{ mV}$).

### 4. Machine-Measurable Acceptance Criteria

| Metric ID | Parameter | Target / Specification | Physical Unit | Measurement Method / Tool |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **AC-01** | Total Bitline Parasitic Capacitance ($C_{BL}$) | $250.0 \pm 12.5$ | $\text{fF}$ | Post-reduction SPICE `.MEASURE` summation of $C_{gnd} + \sum C_c$ along active bitline |
| **AC-02** | Differential Readout Signal ($\Delta V$) | $100.0\text{ to } 150.0$ | $\text{mV}$ | Transient SPICE differential probe $(V_{BL} - V_{\overline{BL}})$ at $t = t_{sense}$ with $C_s = 28\text{ fF}$ |
| **AC-03** | Inter-Bitline Coupling Capacitance Ratio ($C_c / C_{BL}$) | $\le 18.0$ | $\%$ | Reduced matrix extraction ratio: $\left(\frac{\sum C_{c, BL-BL}}{C_{BL}}\right) \times 100\%$ |
| **AC-04** | Maximum Crosstalk Induced Differential Noise ($\Delta V_{xtalk}$) | $\le 25.0$ | $\text{mV}$ | Peak voltage deviation on quiet victim $BL/\overline{BL}$ pair during full-rail aggressor transitions |
