## Overview & Rationale
Precision alignment of the diamond dicing saw blade relative to the scribe lanes (dicing streets) is critical in 180nm DRAM wafer packaging. Scribe streets contain test structures, alignment verniers, and process control monitors (PCM) bordering active DRAM dies. Improper blade offset or stage angular tilt results in die edge chipping, micro-cracks, and severed metallization/seal rings, degrading retention times and yielding functional failure. This SOP details the calibration mechanism using optical split-field registration, spherical air bearing leveling, and closed-loop piezo fine positioning.
