# SOP: I-line Stepper Exposure, Post-Exposure Bake and Development Metrology

- **Date**: 2026-10-12
- **Source**: Sam Zeloof Z1 Garage Semiconductor Journal (2018), BOE Literature Context
- **Status**: Implemented
- **Generation**: Gen 2 (µm)
- **Scope**: DRAM Foundation VN 180nm Open-Source DRAM Chip

## Overview & Rationale
Actionable standard operating procedure for wafer priming, photoresist coating, stepper exposure, development, and metrology inspection for oxide mask patterning.

## Target Parameters & Acceptance Criteria
- HMDS Vapor Prime Temperature: 120°C
- Spin Speed: 4000 RPM (yields ~1.3 µm thickness for Shipley 1813)
- Soft Bake: 115°C for 60 seconds
- Exposure Source: 365nm i-line UV source
- Developer: Microposit MF-319 for 45 to 60 seconds, followed by DI water rinse
- Gen 2 Alignment Tolerance: ±5 µm (tightened from Gen 1 ±100 µm)
