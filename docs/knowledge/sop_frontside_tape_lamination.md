# SOP: Frontside Polyimide / UV-Curable Protective Backgrinding Tape Lamination

- **Date**: 2026-10-15
- **Source**: Agent (Domain Expert AI, DRAM Foundation VN)
- **Status**: Implemented
- **Process Step**: Packaging & Assembly > Frontside Protective Taping & Mechanical Backgrinding to Target Thickness

## Overview & Rationale
Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) for laminating polyimide / UV-curable protective backgrinding tape across the active frontside of 180nm DRAM wafers prior to aggressive mechanical wafer thinning and backgrinding. Ensures zero mechanical abrasion, micro-scratching, or slurry ingress onto the sensitive $100\,\mu\text{m} \times 100\,\mu\text{m}$ aluminum wire bond pads and delicate crown-shaped stacked capacitors.
