## Overview & Scope
Standard Operating Procedure for wiring dynamic driver channels and mapping functional/stress vector stimuli on High-Temperature Operating Life (HTOL) Burn-in Boards (BIB) designed for 180nm DRAM devices. This ensures unattenuated dynamic stress across wordlines ($V_{PP}$ boosted Dickson charge pump circuits), bitlines, and stacked capacitor storage cells at elevated ambient chamber temperatures (125°C).
