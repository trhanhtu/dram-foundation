## Overview & Rationale
Surface hydrophobicity quantification ensures that the transition of native oxide silanol groups (-Si-OH) to hydrophobic methyl-siloxane (-Si-O-Si(CH3)3) monolayer bonds via Hexamethyldisilazane (HMDS) vapor priming is complete. Adequate surface hydrophobicity prevents photoresist delamination/peeling and pinhole capillary lift-off during subsequent wet chemistry etching and high-resolution electron beam direct write (EBDW) lithography.

## Physical Parameters & Operating Points
- Dehydration Bake: 150°C to 200°C for 30 minutes.
- Probe Fluid: Deionized (DI) water (18.2 MOhm·cm resistivity), dispensed volume 2.0 uL to 3.0 uL.
- Target Static Contact Angle ($\theta$): 70° to 85° (hydrophobic silylated surface).
