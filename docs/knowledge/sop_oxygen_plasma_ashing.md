# Downstream Oxygen Plasma Photoresist Ashing

- **Date**: 2026-10-14
- **Source**: Agent (Domain Expert AI)
- **Status**: Accepted

## Overview & Rationale
Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) for stripping cross-linked positive photoresist (Shipley 1813, ~1.3 µm baseline thickness) using downstream RF oxygen plasma at 250 W and 300 mTorr. Downstream plasma eliminates direct energetic ion bombardment of underlying gate oxides and features while utilizing reactive atomic oxygen radicals to volatilize organic resist matrices into CO, CO2, and H2O vapor.

## Key Details / Architecture
- **RF Power**: 250 W.
- **Process Pressure**: 300 mTorr.
- **Feed Gas**: High-purity O2.
- **Target Substrate**: Cross-linked positive photoresist (Shipley 1813, ~1.3 µm nominal thickness).
- **Post-Ash Step**: Deionized (DI) water rinse.
- **Generation Fit**: Gen 1 (mm scale / ±100 µm macroscopic alignment baseline).
