## Overview & Rationale
This standard operating procedure establishes precision setup, mechanical clamping, tool parallelism alignment, and stand-off height calibration for destructive die shear testing of 180nm DRAM dies attached with silver-filled conductive epoxy (Ablebond 84-1LMIS cured at 150°C for 60 min). Proper setup eliminates peel forces, torque vectors, and substrate slippage under MIL-STD-883 Method 2019.

## Key Process & Acceptance Metrics
- Substrate / Package Clamping Rigidity: 0.00 mm lateral displacement during pre-load.
- Tool-to-Die Contact Parallelism: Within <= 1.0 deg (< 5 um gap across die edge).
- Stand-Off Clearance Height: 12.5 um (nominal 10–25 um, <= 13% of die thickness).
- Shear Test Velocity / Loading Rate: Controlled quasi-static displacement 0.1 to 0.5 mm/s.
- Prior Thermal Resistance / C-SAM Quality: R_th <= 5°C/W; void area under die < 5%.
