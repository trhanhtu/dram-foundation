## Overview & Rationale
Detailed standard operating procedure (SOP) for post-CMP Inter-Layer Dielectric (ILD) oxide film metrology in a 180nm DRAM fabrication process. The protocol covers multi-angle spectroscopic ellipsometry data acquisition across a 49-point wafer map, dispersion modeling via the empirical Cauchy formulation, automated full-wafer contour generation, and Within-Wafer Non-Uniformity (WIWNU) statistical verification.
