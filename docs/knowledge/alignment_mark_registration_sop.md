# Global Wafer Alignment Mark Optical/BSE Registration & Coordinate Mapping

- **Date**: 2026-10-15
- **Source**: Agent (Domain Expert AI - DRAM Foundation VN)
- **Status**: Accepted

## Overview & Rationale
Actionable Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) for global wafer alignment mark optical and Backscattered Electron (BSE) registration and coordinate mapping on the EBDW (Electron Beam Direct Write) tool for 180nm open-source DRAM gate patterning.

## Key Details / Architecture
Integrates coarse optical pre-alignment, non-destructive spectroscopic ellipsometry film validation, high-resolution BSE alignment mark registration, and laser interferometer 6-DOF coordinate mapping to enforce SkyWater SKY130 overlay error budgets ($\le 35\text{ nm}$).
