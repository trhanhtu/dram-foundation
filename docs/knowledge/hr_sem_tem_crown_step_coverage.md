# Standard Operating Procedure (SOP): HR-SEM & Cross-Sectional Bright-Field TEM Imaging of High-k Crown Sidewall Step Coverage

- **Date**: 2026-10-15
- **Source**: Domain Expert AI (DRAM Foundation VN)
- **Status**: Implemented
- **Module**: Metrology / Nanofabrication
- **Generation**: Gen 3 (180nm Open-Source DRAM Node)

## Overview & Rationale
In 180nm DRAM architectures utilizing stacked capacitor over bitline (COB) topologies, high-aspect-ratio 3D crown storage nodes require atomic layer deposition (ALD) of high-$k$ dielectrics (such as $\text{Al}_2\text{O}_3/\text{Ta}_2\text{O}_5$) to ensure sufficient storage capacitance ($C_s \ge 25\text{ to }28\text{ fF/cell}$) within an $8F^2$ or $6F^2$ footprint while sustaining cell leakage below $1\text{ fA/cell}$ at $85^\circ\text{C}$. This SOP governs lamella cross-section preparation via DualBeam FIB, low-kV high-resolution in-lens SEM screening, and 200 kV bright-field transmission electron microscopy (BF-TEM) step-coverage and equivalent oxide thickness (EOT) verification along vertical crown sidewalls.

## Key Details / Architecture
- DualBeam FIB sample preparation with electron-beam and ion-beam Pt/C protection layers.
- In-situ Ga+ milling and low-damage Ar/Ga polish down to $<50\text{ nm}$ lamella thickness.
- High-resolution in-lens SEM profile inspection at $1.0\text{ to }2.0\text{ kV}$.
- 200 kV Bright-Field TEM imaging with multi-point calibrated line profiles across Top, Middle, Bottom sidewalls, and Crown bottom corner.
