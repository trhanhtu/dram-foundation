# DRAM Foundation VN - Internet Knowledge Crawler & Ingestion Pipeline

- **Date**: 2026-10-07
- **Source**: User
- **Status**: Accepted

---

## 1. Objective & Strategic Rationale
To ground the AI Council (specifically the **Domain Expert** powered by Gemini) in empirical facts and prevent hallucinations, the local VectorDB cannot rely on static seed data alone. A dedicated **Internet Knowledge Crawler & Ingestion Pipeline** is essential to continuously harvest, clean, and index open-source semiconductor intelligence from the web.

---

## 2. Key Crawling Targets & Intelligence Domains

1. **Grassroots & DIY Silicon Projects**:
   - Sam Zeloof's garage semiconductor fabrication journals (Z1, Z2 processes).
   - LibreSilicon, FreePDK (45nm/180nm), and OpenROAD initiatives.
   - Maker/hacker community reports on DIY spin-coaters, tube furnaces, and vacuum chambers.
2. **Expired Patents & Legacy Architecture**:
   - Expired 180nm/micron DRAM cell patents (1T1C capacitor cell structures, stacked vs. trench capacitors).
   - Historical technical documentation from early semiconductor fabs (e.g., legacy ASML, Nikon, Canon stepper service manuals).
3. **Chemical & Material Processing Data**:
   - Technical Data Sheets (TDS) and Material Safety Data Sheets (MSDS) for photoresists (e.g., Shipley 1813, SU-8).
   - Chemical etch rates (Buffered Oxide Etch / BOE, KOH anisotropic silicon etching).
   - Thermal oxidation growth curves (Deal-Grove model parameters) and diffusion kinetics.
4. **Surplus Hardware & COTS Specs**:
   - Surplus optical lenses, UV LED arrays (365nm / 405nm), precision motorized lead screws, and vacuum pumps.

## 4. Operational Guardrails
- **Format Normalization**: Convert crawled HTML, PDFs, and data tables into modular Markdown chunks with metadata (source URL, publication date, author/org).
- **Offline First**: All crawled data and embeddings persist locally in `knowledge_base/` and ChromaDB so R&D continues fully offline.
- **Provenance Tracking**: Every SOP generated by Gemini must cite the exact source document ID and URL from the crawled database.
