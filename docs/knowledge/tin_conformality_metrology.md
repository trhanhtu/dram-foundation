# SOP: High-Aspect-Ratio Crown TiN Conformality Metrology & Resistance Inspection

- **Date**: 2026-10-15
- **Source**: Agent (Domain Expert AI - DRAM Foundation VN Multi-Agent Council)
- **Status**: Implemented
- **Node**: Gen 3 (180nm DRAM, 8F^2 / 6F^2 COB Topology)

## Overview & Rationale
Detailed standard operating procedure for inline/offline metrology and inspection of atomic layer deposited (ALD) TiN top/bottom metal electrode films lining high-aspect-ratio 3D crown storage capacitors over bitline (COB). Ensures full sidewall/bottom conformality and low sheet resistance required to achieve target capacitance (>28 fF/cell) and sub-1 fA leakage.
