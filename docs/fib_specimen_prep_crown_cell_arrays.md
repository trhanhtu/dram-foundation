# FIB Precision Cross-Sectional Specimen Preparation of Crown Cell Arrays

- **Date**: 2026-10-15
- **Source**: Domain Expert AI (DRAM Foundation VN)
- **Status**: Implemented

## Overview & Rationale
Detailed Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) for Dual-Beam Focused Ion Beam (FIB / SEM) cross-sectional milling and precision specimen preparation of 180nm node high-aspect-ratio 3D Crown Capacitor arrays (with TiN top electrode, Ta2O5/Si3N4 high-k dielectrics, and W-plug contacts).

## Key Details / Architecture
- **In-situ Protective Deposition**: EBID + FIBID Pt / C protective bilayer over Crown capacitor arrays to prevent curtaining and ion implantation damage.
- **Trenching & Cross-Section Polishing**: High-voltage Ga+ coarse milling (30 kV, 1–3 nA) followed by step-down low-kV/low-pA fine polishing (5 kV / 30 kV @ 30–50 pA) to resolve thin-film ALD TiN conformality and dielectric interfaces.
- **Dimensional Verification**: Target pitch alignment with bitline/wordline pitch (360 nm to 400 nm) and access transistor gate length (L = 180 nm).
